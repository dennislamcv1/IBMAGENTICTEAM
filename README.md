# IBMAGENTICTEAM
## IBM Building and Managing Agentic Teams Professional Certificate
### Professional Certificate - 5 course series

The IBM Building and Managing Agentic Teams Professional Certificate prepares leaders and managers to build, deploy, and govern agentic teams where humans and AI agents collaborate to execute work. Across five progressive courses, you will develop practical agentic team leadership skills covering team design, human-AI collaboration, agent configuration in IBM watsonx Orchestrate, multi-agent workflow orchestration, oversight, accountability, trust, responsible AI, and performance management for hybrid teams. You will apply structured frameworks to allocate tasks between humans and agents, define roles and decision rights using an adapted RACI, design for trust and psychological safety, and configure working agents at a manager-configuration level. Through hands-on labs in IBM watsonx Orchestrate, you will orchestrate multi-agent workflows, connect agents to enterprise tools and data, design human handoffs and escalation paths, and build human-in-the-loop oversight proportional to risk. The program addresses governance through the IBM AI Ethics and Trust Framework, teaching you to operationalize ethical commitments into daily team rituals. The capstone produces an integrated leadership package you can present in interviews, internal reviews, and program proposals. No coding background required.
